Clickarr turns a Plex library into television: numbered channels, a program guide, and something always already playing when you turn it on.

Build channels from shows, whole libraries with genre and decade filters, Plex collections, and playlists, or let Clickarr suggest a starting lineup from what you own. Schedules are deterministic, so every TV in the house computes the same program at the same minute without a server in the middle. Tune in at 7:17 and you land seventeen minutes into the episode, like a real channel.

Several TVs can form a household over your own Wi-Fi, paired with a PIN over TLS, or sync through Google Drive for backup and for homes far apart. Your Plex sign-in stays on each TV and is never shared.

Open source (MIT), built in public for Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV. Plex is a trademark of Plex, Inc.; Clickarr is an independent project.
